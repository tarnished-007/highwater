# Store screenshot copy — Highwater

Text to paste into the four Canva slides, replacing the template's placeholder copy.
Character counts are matched to the template's boxes so nothing overflows or
auto-shrinks.

---

## ⚠️ Set the canvas to 1280 × 800 BEFORE designing

The templates you have are **16:9**. Chrome Web Store screenshots must be exactly
**1280 × 800** (8:5) — a 16:9 export gets rejected or letterboxed, and re-cropping
afterwards wrecks the layout.

In Canva: **Custom size → 1280 px × 800 px**, then paste the template elements in.
You gain ~80px of vertical space versus 16:9, so let the headline breathe.

## Brand swaps to make in every slide

| Template has | Change it to |
|---|---|
| Copper/bronze accents (`#B8875A`-ish) | Highwater red — gradient `#FF4D5A` → `#D62438` |
| "LICERIA & CO." + leaf logo | `store/brand/highwater-mark-1024.png` + the word **HIGHWATER** |
| Near-black background | Keep it — it already matches the panel (`#121212`) |
| Body text grey | Keep white/light grey |

Use **`highwater-mark-*.png`** (bars only, transparent) on the dark slides. Use
**`highwater-logo-*.png`** (bars on a dark rounded tile) only on a light background —
on near-black it reads as a floating box.

## Every screenshot needs real UI in it

Google wants screenshots that demonstrate actual functionality. Slide 1 is pure
text, which is weak on its own — drop a real panel screenshot into the empty right
half. Grab the raw captures first (Explore scrolled, panel open, 2–3 chips added),
then place them in the image frames.

## Before you upload

- Exported at exactly **1280 × 800**, PNG or JPEG
- **Blur other people's usernames and avatars**, and your own logged-in account in
  Instagram's UI — these become public
- No claim on any slide that the extension does something it doesn't. It only sorts
  what has loaded; there is no "all of Explore"
